    Explore investing at your own pace

    Start with your goals, access needs and comfort with risk.

    • Purpose and time horizon

      Consider when you may need the money and what you want it to do. Build an investing plan

    • Risk, diversification and costs

      Compare how investments can lose value, how easily you can access them, and their fees. Understand investment types

    • Money you may need soon

      Think about unexpected expenses alongside longer-term goals. Learn about emergency savings

    Compare broad investment types
    • Cash and deposits

      Compare access, withdrawal conditions and interest terms.

    • Bonds

      Understand the issuer and how interest-rate changes can affect value.

    • Shares and funds, including ETFs

      Compare holdings, diversification, fees and exposure to market losses.

    • Superannuation

      Consider investment choices, fees and restrictions on accessing retirement savings.

    • Speculative assets

      Understand volatility and the possibility of losing the money committed.

    These are topics to research, not a ranking or selections for your circumstances. Read ASIC Moneysmart’s comparison
