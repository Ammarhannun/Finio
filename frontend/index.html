    Export a CSV or PDF from your banking app. Finio reads the formats real banks actually export — no bank login, no spreadsheet.

    It cleans the mess and sorts it

    Watch the lines change. CALTEX WOOLWORTHS FUEL is fuel, not groceries. An AI reads every merchant, strips the card-machine noise and files it where it belongs.

  3. 3

    It asks when it genuinely can’t tell

    Money from a friend could be income or just your own transfer. Finio won’t guess at that — it asks a couple of short questions, and remembers your answer forever.

    You see what you actually do

    What you usually earn, spend and keep — averaged over your whole history, not one noisy month. Transfers between your own accounts never count as spending.

Your statement stays yours

The three questions people actually ask before uploading a bank file.

Do you need my bank login?

No. You upload a file you exported yourself. Finio never connects to your bank and never asks for banking credentials.

Who can see my transactions?

Only you. Rows live in a Supabase database with row-level access rules, so one account cannot read another's data.

What does the AI get?

Merchant descriptions, amounts and frequency — never the file itself. It categorises and answers questions; it never tells you what to buy.
