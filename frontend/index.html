How it works

Three steps, about two minutes.

1

Drop in your statement

Export a CSV or PDF from your banking app and upload it. Finio reads the formats real banks actually export.

2

Finio sorts it, and asks when unsure

AI cleans up the merchant names and categorises every transaction. When it genuinely cannot tell what something is, it asks you a handful of short questions instead of guessing.

3

See what you actually do with money

What you usually earn, spend and keep. Where it goes. What is coming. Then ask your coach anything, or tell it to fix something.
