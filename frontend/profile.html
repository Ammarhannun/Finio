AI and your privacy

AI is off until you choose to enable it. Finio can still calculate your figures and use its built-in categorisation and limited coach.

When enabled, selected merchant text, amounts, dates, financial context and chat messages are sent to OpenAI for categorisation, coaching and semantic search. Common identifiers are masked, but some personal information can remain. OpenAI's retention policies apply.

Changes apply to new requests. Saved results, chats and information already sent remain subject to their existing retention policies.
