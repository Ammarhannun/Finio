In detail

Who runs Finio

Finio is operated by Ammar Hannun. The service is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Use the privacy request form below to contact the operator about your data.

Statements and transactions

Finio parses uploaded CSVs and text-based PDFs on its backend. Temporary uploaded files are removed after processing. Parsed transactions, balances, analysis, preferences and coach conversations are stored in the operator's Supabase database, separated by account access rules.

If you enter typical monthly take-home income or a monthly savings target in Profile, Finio stores those planning amounts with your account. They are used for budget comparisons, not treated as a live bank balance or as transactions from your statement.

Spend Check

You can enter a current available balance to calculate a rough purchase estimate. That entered balance is sent to Finio's backend for this calculation but is not saved in check history. Check history does save the item or merchant, purchase amount, date, result and any projected balance.

A bank-statement balance may be out of date; neither estimate is an approval to spend.

AI processing

AI sharing starts off. In Profile, you can choose whether to enable AI features. The backend checks your current account preference before provider requests. With AI off, Finio uses built-in categorisation, calculations and a limited coach. Changes apply to new requests. Existing chats, saved results and information already sent follow their existing retention policies.

When AI is enabled, Finio sends cleaned merchant names, amounts and frequency information to OpenAI for categorisation. Raw bank-description examples are excluded. Coach messages, selected financial context and relevant transaction details may also be sent to answer your questions, including your entered monthly planning amounts when relevant. Merchant names may be embedded for search. Uploaded files themselves are parsed by Finio, not sent as files to the coach.

Before AI requests, Finio masks common email addresses, phone numbers, account and card references, and recognisable street addresses. Unnecessary transaction metadata is excluded. These pattern checks can miss personal names or unusual identifiers. Do not enter bank passwords or security codes. Recognisable credentials are removed from new saved chat messages.

A statement description can include personal information. Only upload data you are comfortable having processed by these services. OpenAI and Supabase apply their own processing and retention policies. Finio disables optional storage of AI chat-completion responses; provider security logs may still be retained.

Retention and control

Finio retains your saved data until you delete it. Profile includes an export and a control to delete saved financial data, goals, budgets, corrections and chats from the live database. Your login account and AI-sharing choice remain available.

Uploading a replacement statement replaces the saved transaction history. Finio asks you to review the date range and confirm this first. Your goals, budgets and correction rules are retained.

Provider backups and operational records may persist according to the operator's provider settings; deleting Finio data does not promise immediate removal from provider backups.

Protecting your account

You can add an authenticator app in Profile for two-step verification. Once enabled, a password alone cannot open your financial data. Keep a secure backup of your authenticator; password recovery does not remove this extra protection.

Downloading or deleting saved data requires a recent sign-in or authenticator check. Finio may ask you to verify again before completing either action.

Privacy requests

Ask about access, correction, deletion or your account. You do not need to sign in to use this form. Messages go to Finio's operator through Resend, not to the AI coach. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Do not include bank statements, account numbers, passwords or security codes. There are no attachments. Your reply email and message are sent to the operator's private inbox. Cloudflare Turnstile checks for automated submissions.

Browser storage and third parties
