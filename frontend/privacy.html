What happens when you upload a statement — in plain terms.

⌂
No bank login. You upload a file. Finio never connects to your bank and never asks for banking credentials.
⚿

In detail

Statements and transactions

Finio parses uploaded CSVs and text-based PDFs on its backend. Temporary uploaded files are removed after processing. Parsed transactions, balances, analysis, preferences and coach conversations are stored in the operator's Supabase database, separated by account access rules.

Spend Check

You can enter a current available balance to calculate a rough purchase estimate. That entered balance is sent to Finio's backend for this calculation but is not saved in check history. Check history does save the item or merchant, purchase amount, date, result and any projected balance.

A bank-statement balance may be out of date; neither estimate is an approval to spend.

AI processing

When AI is enabled, Finio sends merchant descriptions (including examples from the original bank text), amounts and frequency information to OpenAI for categorisation. Coach messages, selected financial context and relevant transaction details may also be sent to answer your questions. Merchant names may be embedded for search. Uploaded files themselves are parsed by Finio, not sent as files to the coach.

A statement description can include personal information. Only upload data you are comfortable having processed by these services. OpenAI and Supabase apply their own processing and retention policies.

Retention and control

Finio retains your saved data until you delete it. Profile includes an export and a control to delete saved financial data, preferences and chats from the live database. Your login account remains available.

Uploading a replacement statement replaces the saved transaction history. Finio asks you to review the date range and confirm this first. Your goals, budgets and correction rules are retained.

Provider backups and operational records may persist according to the operator's provider settings; deleting Finio data does not promise immediate removal from provider backups.

Browser storage and third parties

Your browser stores sign-in/session information and display preferences. The frontend loads fonts and JavaScript libraries from external content providers. The sample dashboard uses synthetic data and does not require a bank statement.
